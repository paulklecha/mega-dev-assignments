Not one bug reported many times. Two bugs, with two different fixes, merged on subject alone.

The table is built at render time from the export, so the counts stay honest. Intake's tag is not the cause: it guesses from the reporter's browser the moment the report arrives, and it is wrong often enough to hide a whole bug. The cause is computed from the tenant's locale and form.

The two problems

1 · parser — 379 reports. The shared parser reads every date month-first. Every tenant writes day-first, so 7 April ships as 4 July. This reaches people on every channel (app, email, form), across all 11 tenants. This is what #4471 is really about. Ticket: parser reads dates month-first.

2 · hint — 33 reports. A different bug, merged into #4471 by subject. The booking-v3 form prints the hint MM/DD/YYYY. Its tenants are day-first, so their people type the date month-first as told, and the form stores the wrong date at entry, before the parser runs. It shows up on the form channel only, for the three booking-v3 tenants (alder.physio, copper.studio, pale.harbour). Fixing the parser would not fix it. Ticket: booking-v3 form hint stores wrong date.

Resolved (desk, #4471 closed 05 Oct)

  • parser — FIXED. fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. Ticket #1 closed as completed.
  • hint — WON'T FIX. booking-v3 form retired, so the form-hint bug goes away with the form. Ticket #2 closed as not planned.

Count once, but count the right things

  • The export had 427 rows; its pages overlap, so 15 were the same report twice. Counted by id, #4471 holds 412 reports.
  • Split by cause: 379 parser + 33 hint = 412.
  • Five reports the desk read as a one-day move are still parser by cause; they are footnoted, not split out.

Lessons

  • Merging on subject is fast, but it buries a second bug with the same symptom. Re-read the merge when a "known" ticket keeps growing.
  • A number that can be computed should be computed. The intake tag disagreed with the cause on 38 reports; trusting it would have split the counts wrong.
